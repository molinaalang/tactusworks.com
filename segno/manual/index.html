Segno help

Questions or problems: write to segnohelp@gmail.com. Please say which phone or tablet you use.

Not sure what a button does? Hold your finger on it and Segno tells you.

Add a passage

On the main page, choose Take a photo, Scan a page, Choose photos or Open a PDF. Segno finds the lines of music on the page by itself, on your device.

If it says it couldn't find the staves, hold the phone square to the page, get a little closer, and try again.

To add more pages to a passage, open it and tap Add pages. New pages go at the end.

To join two parts into one, hold your finger on a passage in the library and choose Combine with….

Mark a section

In the reader, tap Mark, then tap the first line of the section and then its last. Drag the START and END handles to adjust. Give it a name and choose Save, or Save & practice to start reading it straight away.

Reading

Tap the sides of the music or swipe to move on. Bluetooth page-turner pedals work too.

With the phone sideways, Segno shows up to three lines at a time. Pinch to zoom in until one line fills the screen; each turn then moves one screen along the line, then on to the next line. You can switch between page view and one line under Hands-free, Layout.

Page turns has seven styles. The showy ones are for a whole page on an iPad or larger tablet held upright. On a phone the lines glide.

The first-use tour is in Settings, under Show tips again.

Hands-free turns

In the reader, tap Hands-free.

Nod to turn: lift your chin a little to turn the page. Nod size sets how big a nod it waits for. It uses the front camera and works with glasses. It is off until you switch it on.

Say "next" / "back": Segno listens on the device for those two words. It needs the microphone and speech recognition, and is off until you switch it on.

If you turned the camera or microphone off for Segno, turn it back on in your device's Settings app, under Segno.

Metronome, tuner and recorder

Metronome is in the reader bar. Pick a groove or a plain click, and tap the tempo in with Tap tempo. While a groove plays you can raise your eyebrows and the drums play a fill (Eyebrow fill, in the metronome panel).

Tuner is at the top right of the main page and in the reader bar. It sounds an A from 415 to 446 Hz.

Record keeps your last three takes of each passage. Star a take to keep it for good, or hold one to delete it.

Pen and highlighter

Tap Annotate in the reader, or tap the music with two fingers. Your markings show in every view. Apple Pencil works on iPad.

Export a PDF

Open a passage and choose Export PDF. Every page comes out with your markings, ready for Files or another score reader.

Sync

On iPhone and iPad, Settings, iCloud sync keeps your music the same on all your devices. It uses your own iCloud account.

On Android you can switch on sync to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, over Wi-Fi only if you like.

Delete a passage

Hold your finger on it in the library and choose Delete. This removes the pages and your markings and can't be undone.

Make it yours

Settings has eight colour themes, the stick-man visitor, sounds and practice breaks. In the reader you can pick from 21 backgrounds to sit behind the music.
