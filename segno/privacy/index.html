Segno privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Segno is a sheet-music practice app for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Tactus Works (Alan Molina). This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Segno has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. I don't run any servers, and I never see your music, photos, recordings or anything else you put in the app. Everything is processed on your device. If you turn on sync, your library is stored in your own iCloud or Google Drive account, which only you can read.

What stays on your device

The photos you take or import, the passages Segno crops from them, your pen and highlighter markings, your recordings and your settings are all stored inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera

Segno uses the camera to photograph sheet music. If you switch on hands-free page turns, it also uses the front camera to notice a chin lift or an eyebrow raise. Those camera frames are read on the device as they arrive and are never saved or sent anywhere. Hands-free turns are off until you switch them on.

Microphone and speech

Segno uses the microphone to record you playing, only when you press record. Recordings stay on your device unless you star one and have sync turned on (see below).

On iPhone and iPad, voice page turns listen for "next" and "back". Speech recognition is set to run on the device, so your voice is not sent to Apple or anyone else. Voice turns are off until you switch them on.

Photo library

On iPhone and iPad, Segno reads only the photos you pick to import. It does not look through the rest of your library.

Sync

On iPhone and iPad, your library and starred recordings sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. Apple's privacy policy covers that storage, and I can't access it.

On Android, sync is optional. If you sign in with Google, Segno stores your library and starred recordings in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Segno can only see the files it put there, not the rest of your Drive. Google's privacy policy covers that storage, and I can't access it. You can remove the folder's data from Google Drive's settings under "Manage apps".

Google ML Kit (Android)

On Android, Segno uses Google's ML Kit face detection to notice a chin lift. It runs on the device and does not send camera images to Google. Google says ML Kit may send it basic information about how the library is performing, such as its version and errors, to keep it working.

Children

Segno doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
