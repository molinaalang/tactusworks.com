Where it came from

I’m an orchestral violinist. I was learning a rhythmically complex contemporary piece when the idea for Tactus came to me.

I wanted to see and hear the bigger rhythmic picture, and to separate the complex rhythms from the notes so I could isolate them in practice. Every tool I tried had too much friction.

Tactus is the tool I wanted. I use it often in my work as a professional musician.
