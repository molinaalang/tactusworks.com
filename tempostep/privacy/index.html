Privacy policy.

The short version: TempoStep does not collect your data, does not need an account, and sends nothing about you or your practice anywhere.

Effective date: November 26, 2026  ·  TempoStep 2.0

What we collect.

In short

Nothing. TempoStep does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

TempoStep runs on your device. It does not have a server backend, does not create user accounts, and does not send your information off your device. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, and no advertising framework embedded in the app.

The mic stays on your device.

TempoStep uses the microphone to listen for silence between practice passes. It detects when you stop playing and uses that to advance the tempo.

The microphone audio is processed entirely on-device in real time. No audio is recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere. The app listens only for the presence or absence of sound above a calibrated threshold; it does not capture or store what you play.

You can switch to Manual mode at any time to disable mic detection entirely. In Manual mode the microphone is not accessed.

The app asks for microphone permission the first time you press start with listening on. On iPhone and iPad you can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Your data stays on your device.

  • No account required. TempoStep does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information.
  • No analytics. The app does not include any analytics or tracking library. Usage patterns, session lengths, and feature interactions are not measured or reported.
  • No advertising. There are no ads in the app, and no advertising identifiers are accessed.
  • One network request. On iPhone and iPad, the list of other Tactus Works apps in Settings is downloaded from tactusworks.com, at most once a day. The request carries none of your data. Your settings are stored on your device and the app does not send them anywhere.
  • No crash reporting SDK. If the app crashes, nothing is sent to us automatically. If you experience a problem, we welcome a report by email.

The App Store handles payment.

TempoStep is a one-time purchase sold through the Apple App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store any payment information, credit card details, or purchase history. Apple's privacy policy governs that transaction.

Safe for all ages.

TempoStep collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

This website.

This website, tactusworks.com, counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It records which pages are viewed, which site sent you here, and your country, browser and type of device, and shows them only as totals. It sets no cookies and does not follow you onto other sites. The app sends nothing to Cloudflare. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at tactusworks.com/tempostep/privacy/ with a revised effective date. We do not collect email addresses, so we cannot notify you directly. Check this page for changes.

Questions.

If you have any questions about this policy or the app's data practices, email us at tempostepapp@gmail.com. We'll respond as soon as we can.
